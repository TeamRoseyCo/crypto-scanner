## WHY THIS WORKS (the reasoning for future reference)

1. **It's transformative, not reproductive.** You don't read their text. You
   reference two specific data points (STH cost basis structure, $1.35B
   realized losses) and use them to support YOUR system's regime call. That's
   fair use commentary.

2. **It's short.** 45 seconds inside a 12-15 minute video means viewers
   don't tune out for a tangent — they get a quick "external validation"
   moment then back to the main thread.

3. **It positions you correctly.** You're not "the guy who reads Glassnode
   to you." You're "the guy who built a scanner and corroborates it with
   on-chain data when the regime call matters." Different and stronger.

4. **It models good practice for viewers.** A lot of crypto YouTube either
   makes wild claims with no source, OR reads other people's reports without
   adding anything. This shows what *actual analysis* looks like — taking
   one credible source, picking the relevant data point, and connecting it
   to a concrete decision.
