# YOUTUBE STRATEGY — PATH A (educational long-form, monetization-focused)

**Created:** 2026-06-02
**Goal:** Build an audience that compounds toward YouTube monetization
**Target:** 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours in 12 months (Partner Program threshold)
**Stretch:** $1,000/month ad revenue within 18-24 months

---

## WHY THE PREVIOUS APPROACH WASN'T WORKING

Channel state as of 2026-06-02:
- 38 videos uploaded
- 8 subscribers
- 1.49K total views (~39 views per video)
- Zero verified engagement on auto-uploaded content

Diagnosis:
1. **Daily auto-uploaded Shorts** have $0.05-$0.30 CPM. To make $1,000/month
   at $0.10 CPM you'd need 10 million Shorts views per month. Math doesn't work.
2. **"Today's scanner picks"** expires in 24 hours. Not searchable, not evergreen.
3. **Auto-generated face-less daily content** doesn't build viewer relationships.
   YouTube's algorithm rewards return viewers — daily disposable content doesn't.
4. **Unverified claims** in hooks ("LAB pumped 34%", "GMT 47%") risk credibility
   if challenged in comments.
5. **Mismatched titles/visuals** (GMT video showing XLM chart) tells YouTube
   "low quality" and depresses recommendations.

The auto-pipeline produces faster than humans can find reasons to watch.

---

## PATH A: ONE LONG-FORM VIDEO PER WEEK, MANUALLY MADE

Everything in this section is the strategy. The auto-pipeline stays OFF.

### What changes
- **Frequency:** 1 video per week (not 7)
- **Length:** 10-15 minutes (not 60s Shorts)
- **Format:** Educational deep-dives on the scanner system you built
- **Production:** Manual. You record, you script, you publish
- **Topic structure:** evergreen explainers, not daily picks

### Why this works
1. **Long-form CPM is 5-30x higher** than Shorts ($2-$6 vs $0.05-$0.30)
2. **Evergreen content compounds.** A good "How does RSI divergence work" video
   keeps pulling views 12 months later. A daily Shorts video gets 30 views and dies.
3. **You have a genuine edge:** you built a 21-indicator scanner system. Most
   crypto YouTubers can't claim that. That's the asymmetric content nobody else has.
4. **Manual production builds personality.** Even faceless, your phrasing and
   perspective become recognizable. The algorithm rewards recognizable voices.
5. **One video a week is sustainable** for 6-12 months. Daily isn't.

### Production budget
- 4-6 hours per video, broken into: script (1h), record (1h), edit (2h), thumbnail (30min), publish (30min)
- That's about half a day per week of focused work, not a daily ritual

## THE STARTER VIDEO

**Make video #1 first. ("I built a 21-indicator crypto scanner. Here's what's
actually in it.")**

Why this one:
- It IS your channel. Everything else builds on it.
- It's the only video you can make where nobody else has the angle
- You have the entire `indicators.py` file as your script source
- The audience for this video pre-selects for "people who want depth in crypto
  scanners" — exactly the audience that converts to subscribers
- The video is partly already written (your existing audit output + signals.py
  comments)

Production approach for video #1:
1. **Script:** 2-3 hours. Use `indicator_audit.py --verbose` output as your
   outline. For each indicator, write 30-90 seconds: what it is, why it's
   in your scanner, what it catches.
2. **Visuals:** Screen recording of running the audit + indicators.py code
   + 1-2 charts showing each indicator at work. No animations needed.
3. **Recording:** Your voice (not TTS). Even average human voice beats TTS
   for retention. ElevenLabs clone is OK as a fallback if you genuinely
   can't record, but YOU recording is better.
4. **Editing:** Cut pauses, keep it tight at 12-15 min. Don't pad.
5. **Title:** "I Built a 21-Indicator Crypto Scanner — Here's What's Inside"
   (simple, descriptive, searchable)
6. **Thumbnail:** screenshot of the audit output with one indicator name
   circled + "21 INDICATORS" overlay text. Don't overthink it.
7. **Description:** what the scanner does, link to subsequent videos in the
   series ("Next: how funding rates create the second edge").
8. **Pinned comment:** "Which indicator should I deep-dive on first? Drop
   one in the replies."

Publish manually. Watch the analytics. The first video is the data point
that tells you whether Path A is viable.

## DECISION TREE — WHAT TO DO BASED ON VIDEO #1 RESULTS

After 14 days, the starter video will have a clear signal.

### IF it gets >300 views with positive watch-time retention:
- Path A is working. Make video #2.
- Stay on the weekly cadence.
- Build the series — each subsequent video builds context from earlier ones.

### IF it gets 50-300 views with decent retention:
- Mixed signal. The content is right but discovery is the gap.
- Try one more video (Tier 2 — RSI divergence or Funding rates).
- After video #2, reassess. If still 50-300, the format needs different
  promotion (X cross-posting, niche subreddit shares, Reddit AMAs in
  r/CryptoCurrency or r/algotrading).

### IF it gets <50 views with poor retention:
- Path A in this form isn't reaching anyone.
- Two options:
  - (a) Adjust the format (different opening hook, more visual cuts, shorter)
  - (b) Pivot to Path C (channel as archive, drop monetization goal)
- Don't make a third video without changing something deliberate.

## METRICS THAT MATTER (NOT VANITY METRICS)

After each video, look at:

1. **Average view duration** (target: >50% of video length)
2. **Click-through rate from impressions** (target: >4%)
3. **Subscriber gain from this video** (target: >0.5% of views)
4. **Watch-time hours added** (counts toward 4K threshold)
5. **Comments asking real questions** (signal of engaged audience)

Ignore (for now):
- Likes, view count alone, hashtag impressions, "trending" position
