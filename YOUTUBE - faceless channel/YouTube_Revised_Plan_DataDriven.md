## SHORT RETENTION FIX (current avg: 17% → target: 50%+)

Your Shorts are losing viewers in the first 2–3 seconds. Fix:

1. **First frame must be the chart** — no intro, no channel name, no "hey guys"
2. **Text overlay on first frame:** bold claim like "XDC JUST HIT 8.0 ON MY SCANNER"
3. **Voice starts immediately** — first word of narration on frame 1
4. **Cut length to 30–45 seconds max** — your 27s video ("I almost missed this ALT move") had 15.4% retention vs your 60s videos averaging 10–12%
5. **End with an open loop:** "Full breakdown in the long video — link in comments"
6. **No generic intros.** No "what's up guys." No "in today's video." Start with the signal.

## THUMBNAIL FORMULA (based on your data)
